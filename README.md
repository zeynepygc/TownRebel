# Town Rebel

A 2D endless runner built in Unity, made as part of Unity's **Junior Programmer** learning pathway. Control a punk character who auto-runs through a town, jumping over incoming obstacles — hit one, and it's game over.

▶️ **Play it here:** (https://play.unity.com/en/games/287b5d83-31cb-4992-b869-e14fced54874/town-rebel)


## Gameplay

- The player character runs automatically from left to right.
- Press **Spacebar** to jump over obstacles in your path.
- Colliding with an obstacle ends the run.
- The background scrolls endlessly to simulate movement, and obstacles spawn at timed intervals.

## Features

- **Core mechanics** — jump input, obstacle spawning via a spawn manager, and collision-based game over state.
- **Seamless scrolling background** — the environment repeats infinitely to keep the illusion of continuous movement, and halts on game over.
- **Character animation** — dedicated running, jumping, and death animations, timed to match the game's pacing.
- **Audio & particle effects** — sound effects and particles trigger on running, jumping, and crashing, adding feedback and polish to player actions.

## Built With

- **Unity** (C#)
- ShaderLab (Unity shader files)

## Project Background

This project was built by following Unity's **Junior Programmer** pathway, across four main stages:

1. **Core Gameplay Setup** — project setup, importing starter assets, choosing a background and player character, implementing jump input, and building a spawn manager for obstacles.
2. **Endless Background & Game Over Logic** — fixing the disappearing background by making it loop seamlessly, halting the game on collision, and cleaning up obstacles that pass the player.
3. **Character Animation** — adding run, jump, and death animations, and tuning their timing to fit the game.
4. **Sound & Particle Effects** — adding sound effects and particle effects for running, jumping, and crashing to bring the game to life.
